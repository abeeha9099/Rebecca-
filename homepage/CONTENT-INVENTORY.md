## Other issues found on the live site

- **Broken footer link:** the San Diego number links to `http://tel:8582854315`.
- **Malformed call link:** the top bar's Encinitas number uses `tel:(760) 239-0845`.
- **Property Division:** the homepage card links to a placeholder (`#linkit`), so the demo routes it to the contact page.
- **Child Support card:** the text says support is paid "from the custodial parent to the non-custodial parent", which looks reversed. Not used.
- **Outcome wording:** "committed to ensuring the best outcome for your family" and "will obtain the best results" could read as promises. Not used.
- **Dated content:** the privacy policy is dated 2015, and the awards listed are from 2016 or earlier.
- **Hidden headings:** the page code still holds headings not shown in visible text, including "COVID-19 Update" and "Learn about Divorce Options™ Workshop!". Visible text uses "Divorce Options®".
