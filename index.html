Four ways forward, one conversation to start.

Mediation and collaborative law each offer a consensual, non-adversarial approach that encourages constructive communication and creative problem-solving.

Talk Through My Options
[01]MediationOne neutral

Both of you meet with a trained family law mediator, who acts as a neutral third party and helps you reach compromises. Those compromises are then formalized in a written agreement.

[02]Collaborative PracticeTeam-based

You do not need to appear before a judge. Each spouse works with a team of professionals to settle all aspects of the divorce or separation by written agreement.

[03]Legal ConsultingAdvice on hand

Mediation-friendly legal consulting, parenting plan assistance, or a second opinion and strategy session, for when you want guidance on a specific decision.

[04]Court RepresentationWhen necessary

When a matter does need to go before a judge, Rebecca can assist with court proceedings related to your divorce, such as spousal and child support.
